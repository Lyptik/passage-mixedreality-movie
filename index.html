About the film

Virtual worlds can be
marvelous or dangerous.

PASSAGE shows two individuals uploading their minds in surreal worlds and getting lost in the layers.

▷ Watch the movie

This movie has been shot with a very specific mixed reality technique. All the visuals have been directly captured “in camera” and do not contain elements added after the shooting.

Working with immersive experiences for more than 10 years, creative studio THÉORIZ started to explore how they could apply this approach to cinema. In 2016, they launched the internally called “mixed reality rnd project”. One year later, the early results in their first released video went quickly viral on internet.
